Maidhen

a pixelated space odyssey

Currently, Maidhen's source code and assets were recovered by decompiling an old APK; they await in a drawer the day they will be reassembled to bring the game back to life.

Maidhen, the ultimate retro space shooter.

Take control of the Maidhen spacecraft and travel through a hostile and pixelated universe. Combat a great variety of enemy ships and epic bosses in this fast-paced arcade experience.

Epic Space Battles

Face waves of enemies in intense shoot-em-up action. Master your weapons and dodge patterns to survive to cosmic onslaught.

Challenging Bosses

Test your skills against massive boss encounters. Learn their attack patterns and strike at the perfect moment to claim victory.

Retro Pixel Art

Experience authentic pixel art graphics that pay homage to classic arcade shooters. Every sprite crafted with nostalgic detail.
